# Kinetic revision: noise becomes three replies

Reference review, October 14, 2026. The film keeps its real light-theme UI and
Inter Tight display type. The revised graphic sections share one idea: hundreds
of unread messages become three people who need a reply. The existing demo's
Respond category already contains three messages.

## Sources and interpretation

- [DIA — Mailchimp motion system](https://dia.tv/project/mailchimp/).
  The case study ties brand motion to product behavior and a consistent type
  system. Our interpretation: a scanning selection locks onto “you?”, and the
  brand reveal assembles in three horizontal bands. No DIA artwork, typeface,
  mascot, or animation footage is included. The case-study text was inspected;
  its video did not play in the in-app browser.
- [Ordinary Folk — Intro](https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/project/ordinary-folk),
  creative direction Jorge R. Canedo E.; design Yuki Yamada, Stephanie Stromenger,
  and Jorge R. Canedo E. The official film was opened and its shape-to-mark
  transformation inspected. Our interpretation: the inbox's existing dot matrix
  reorganizes into a large numeral 3, then collapses into the reveal. The ending
  resolves three mail-shaped fields into three dots. All geometry is original.
- [BUCK — Notion: Think It. Make It.](https://buck.co/work/notion-think-it-make-it),
  with Notion illustrator Roman Muradov; animation lead Vero Gómez. The campaign
  uses a conceptual story around the product rather than relying on UI alone.
  The official campaign page, rollout film and campaign images were inspected.
  Our interpretation: make the privacy promise spatial by drawing a computer
  window around the words. No characters or Notion assets are borrowed.
